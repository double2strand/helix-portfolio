7 August 2025 | Life, Poetry

Esplanades...

I      Too many roads there are to take;

But mine is the one that no feet rake.

There is no time and a tale to tell,

Before the curfew, before the death-knell.

I walk this heavenly esplanade,

I was left alone, with none to aid.

I was left alone, but I walked on;

Only to see witherto; had she gone …….

II     Too many winds, there are, that blow;

But the zephyr ‘tis; with youthful onflow,

The augur may be bleak but there’s a tale to tell.

Before the last caterwaul, before the last yell.

I walk this moorish esplanade,

I was borne upon the wind’s serenade.

I was left alone; but I walked on;

Only to see witherto, had she gone …….

III   Too many seas, there are, to cross;

The lonely gull; the vast albatross:-

To ken; are they, but there’s a tale to tell.

Before the storm, before I am fell.

I walk on Triton’s esplanade,

I was borne upon the wave’s renegade,

I was left alone; but I walked on;

Only to see witherto, had she gone ……..

IV    Too many arduous steps to climb;

But fall may my arms, or perhaps a limb.

I should stay the strides; but there’s a tale to tell.

Before the last fall before the gates of hell.

I walk this cemented esplanade,

I was borne by the sidelong balustrade.

I was left alone; but I walked on;

Only to see witherto, had she gone ……..

V     My paths do not divide; they do not split,

My roads are coarse and my feet have bled;

But I walk on with immeasurable grit:-

My perception is weak, blotted by red.

But is this sunrise or sunset?

VI    I have stepped oft, onto these sandy strips,

But never has the Adam’s ale been this obstreperous.

As this nectar washes over me in euphonic drips,

My wounds are harsh, but all too desirous.

Yet is this war or perhaps, tranquility?

VII   I meander through these woods,

I tread gently, on my quest for the questions.

I catechize to the canopy and its consummate hoods.

Answers I shall seek, but on heavenly bastions.

And the answers I shall have at the end of my spectrum.

VIII  I dive into these chasms that, thus; draw my soul,

With arms wide open, I fall through this cloud;

Charmed by an angel of Utopia; for this is no ghoul.

An eternity is spent in the felicity of this shroud.

But are these the rains of heaven or the storms of inferno?

IX    As the storms have begun to breed,

I stay tethered to the mist of hopes; that are mine.

The clouds are dying and the love is an evanescent creed.

Silted sands sear my feet in my quest for the divine.

Am I buried in the macabre sepulchre of my hope……..?
