8 August 2025 | Life, Poetry

Spasms of Life

Shot through the arm or shot through the heart,

An affliction is borne just before we depart.

Spasms for the augur and spasms of today,

Equally I entail on every single day.

On losing a friend, strikes the pain,

Being lovelorn is no lesser a bane.

No sleep I get, my eyes are red,

A spasm is a feeling of utter dread.

Some throes are just beyond description,

When Cupid makes his deep inscription.

When Elysian turns one near and dear,

When throngs the heart, with pervasive fear.

For the warrior of iron stature,

Infinitesimal is every pang of nature.

For those who have a resplendent wisdom,

Pains bring nothing other than boredom.

The dreams which no man ever dreams,

O my friend! Those are my realms.

With a spasm which gives me life,

I drink that nectar without strife.

No chant of the bird on the bough,

No opera, no seed of the ballad’s sow.

Has told ever a tale so dolorous,

Or seeped a venom as poisonous.

A spasm is a pleasure among feelings,

Not the simplest of courageous dealings.

Cut open; mortal may be the wound,

But nearby isn’t there a bloodhound?

Bloody are the battles of the heart,

Love is deadliest of all the archer’s darts.

Chariots of fire, racing each other,

Why does brother give pain to brother?

Throes of refusal and pangs of denial,

O, which one is more infernal?

Shatter all bloodlines, crash the needles-

That prick you as machiavellian beetles.

A warrior I am, mortally cut,

Heart or vein, can’t say, but-

Deep within, scream many spasms,

Largest among the earth’s chasms.
