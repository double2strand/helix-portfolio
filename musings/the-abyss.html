7 August 2025 | Life, Poetry

The Abyss

Gentle at times and turbulent at others,

The vast plain of azure and cerulean.

Peaceful vanity and arrogance like brothers.

Bridging its dimensions, is a task herculean.

The seltzer gently thrashes the land,

Brimming with life and vivacity.

Reducing the rocks to ethereal sand,

Frolicking with amazing veracity.

Multitudes of volume and pulchritude of shape;

Sweet nectar overflowing from vineyards of grape.

Wavy tendrils rising to high a celestial rostrum;

Supporting diverse every creature; otter or oppossum.

As the tender dawn strikes through the mist and shroud,

Many a gull breaks through the silver lining of the cloud.

Amidst the azure frolicks the snow white foam,

Beyond which stretches the impalpable plain of loam.

On a night of utmost serendipity;

Comes a time when the seltzer gets gritty.

Seeking vengeance on a machiavellian spectre,

The water billows like a raptor of ere.

With a sense of fury, unimaginable,

Follows a battle of ferocity, unattainable.

Oh! Who is the nemesis; it is for all to ken.

Yet none can perceive, neither beast nor men.

Then why is there such a pervasive sound;

As though there is close by a hellish bloodhound.

The universal pacifier is now a burning crucible,

A force of might and power invincible.

But yet again, ere the dawn must dawn,

The marine is back with its cerulean lawn.

And yet again as the dusk delves deep;

A turbulent wraith will silently creep.

With golden tresses upon an azure frame,

Oh! Wherefrom hail do you, my dainty dame.

Ere, the massive world was born.

The abyss was here, lovely and lovelorn.
