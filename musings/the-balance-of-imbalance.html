The Balance of Imbalance

Nature has existed for eons now, and for an infinitesimal fraction of that, so has humanity in all its farcical glory. With the fleeting ravages of time, mankind has articulated towering constructs of science, philosophy and the most absurdly imposing among them – religion.

With this pretence of understanding everything around us in highly intricate terms, comes the vapid belief of our being a superior species. An all-encompassing belief that is as hollow as it is blindingly iridescent. And yet while thriving as the most prized species on this planet, our lives happen to be rife with indecision & debased empathy for the nature that bore us and a fundamentally sickening need to find mirth in the dearth of the same for those living around us – flora, fauna and man. At the crooked crux of it all lies the eternal inability of humanity to appreciate one simplistic concept – Imbalance.

Why narrow it down to this one seemingly innocuous word, one might ask. Yet the plethora of how imbalance tilts and dictates the scales of life is not something that can be ignored. For instance, humanity prides itself on its ability to emote and express those emotions in the context of relationships. And yet, what does one define a relationship as? The fix for solitude perhaps. But then solitude can never be truly gotten rid of. For every day as the lights are cast out, everyone can but seek solace only in the solitude of one’s mind. Who defines the contours between complacence and comfort in such relationships then? Perhaps there are none purely because perfection is the shroud that we all seek in the veiled desperation to find balance. Therefore, our ability to emote is imbalanced because we forever seek the illusion of balance existing.

This crucible of imbalance spills over bit by bit on to everything humanity touches. We call it power struggles, depression, anxiety – a million metaphors for our failure of accepting what has eternally been presenting to us at every juncture of our progress. Take the employer and the employee, take the politico and the populace, take science and religion – the scales are always tilted in the favour of balance being tilted. We create art which struggles to find balance between being abstract and articulate. We create music that threshes between cacophony and melody. We create rules that set into motion the wheel of imbalance between order and rebellion.

The funny side to all of this?

Science, the ‘prodigal heir’ to humanity and its rationality designs a term for this victory of imbalance over balance – Entropy, or the degree of disorder as laymen know it. Science, which we claim to be our saviour, defines and whole-heartedly defines the imbalance that defines life itself, a dictum which we have always failed to accept. And hence, the ‘inspired’ pursuit of balance continues for us humans, and so does our descent into the oblivion that awaits our vain, surrogate supremacy.

Nowhere is this more prevalent than in this headless diatribe that seeks balance between cynicism and rationality.

Surely, if irony had to choose a metaphor for ignorance – it would be humanity.
