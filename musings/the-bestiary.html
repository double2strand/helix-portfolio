The Bestiary

The Lion is the King of the Jungle,

This is perhaps a fact that no one can jumble.

But, here comes a tale that you all will remember;

From the 1st of Jan., to the end of December.

The Lord of the Jungle had, had a nasty tumble,

He had no youthful heir, neither a consort humble.

The Tiger, his striped commander-in-chief,

Stood beside his king in inconsolable grief.

The duty-bound King breathed his last,

And the Tiger went on to carry out his orders, fast.

The bestiary was to be alerted in order to avoid anarchy.

A new king was to be chosen before there was any malarkey.

The Chief of Mail was the benevolent pigeon.

He sent out his messengers to every single region.

And suddenly during the day, one could witness night.

As the owls and doves blocked the skies in their flight.

And then started arriving the greatest of the animals,

The majestic elephants and the hyenas who looked like cannibals.

Then came the ravens and a murder of crows,

And also the great antelopes in royal orders and rows.

The splendid swans with a penchant for jewellery,

And a rout of wolves, who were masters in devilry.

The panthers and the leopards that flew over the ground.

The hounds, with their blood red eyes left everyone astound.

The Kings of the skies, the hawks and the eagles,

And from the seas yonder, came the largest of seagulls.

The vultures came too; accompanied by the odd sparrow.

And also the swift, that shot across like an arrow.

“Here come the great mariners”, announced the lark.

And indeed emerged a vessel and abaft was the shark.

There were the squids and also the whales.

Schools of tuna efficiently handled the sails!

It was indeed a grand association,

Between the princes of every nation,

And a royal, splendid banquet took place;

Where the serpents played the music, and the mongoose danced with grace.

During this session, unnoticed by all;

Slipped in the monkeys, both short and tall.

They looked so meek and yet so clever.

That none could guess their intentions, ever.

“Let’s stop the party, guys”, the Tiger said.

“It’s time now to choose a new ruler instead.”

And they began a serious round of debating.

With many creatures coming forth with claims exhilarating.

As the elephants were speaking, there erupted loud sounds of screeching.

And all creatures ran amok; by every code of conduct, breaching.

The whole forest erupted in a wild conflagration.

And the monkeys who were pleased; gazed on in admiration.

In the entire confusion, the creatures banged against each other,

And the rage that erupted, resulted in the killing of brother by brother.

There was a terrible holocaust, and the animals went mad.

The end of the story, was as usual very sad.

The skies had erupted and the waters turned bloody.

The earth had never seen a battle so ruddy.

This great assembly that had gathered to decide;

Now lay as corpses, ripped from side to side.

The inceptuous monkeys partied in bliss,

Upon the remains of this great meeting that went amiss.

They were not sorry, but were jumping in glee.

As they had become the new rulers of the bestiary.

A huge gorilla ascended the royal seat.

As the rhesus monkeys beat a loud retreat.

All prudence was entirely forgotten.

As the forest came to be ruled by a ruler so rotten.

You have all certainly hearkened to this tale.

Now, one fact you must comprehend without fail.

It is not always necessary.

That the lion must rule the bestiary.
