The Mystic

Oh eternal wanderer of the misty lands;

Where dost thou head to?

Oh nomadic dame of utmost beauty

Tread so soft that the ground shan't yield

For thou must be pacific on this arduous field.

Farther than seas of summer yore,

Entailing your satchel of pulchre and lore.

The devilish ice will question with rationale,

Why will  thy not your life lend?

Thus thou must be pacific at this crafty bend.

Life, all polar and marine, hides amidst the white,

But none will appear upon this glen.

No intent machiavellian, and yet there is neither farm nor fen.

With every single ounce of grit

Thou must be pacific or the ice may split.

Now, bobbing upon the seas that bear the zephyr,

A cerulean image thou art flecked by foamy sapphire

The dark and devilish will hungrily prey;

Upon the naivety of the fish and cray.

But what will thy do when the Adam's ale is grey?

When advance do the gargantuan forces of the lido,

And as the cormorant plunges to plague the azure,

The ale rises as a pervasive entity.

Chicanery sets in as the waves roar about.

But what will thy do when the water is in reel and rout?

The winds that entail the extremities of disaster.

Bring naught but a message that is sent from the farthest aster.

The tide will not hearken or stop to the woe signs.

And how will thy then cross the endless azure

Without any aid from a goddess demure?

Then in the end the lido is overcome and the storm is spent.

Many smiles are then broken and many prayers divine sent.

Shores mysterious are fathomed in glory.

But where art thou life, on this island so gory?
