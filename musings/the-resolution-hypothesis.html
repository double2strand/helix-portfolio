The Resolution Hypothesis

Resolutions. A word that has now perhaps inundated the day to day jargon of the modern youth, comes into gargantuan prominence at the onset of a new year… And of course a word that is closely intertwined with the student community; more specifically in this case with the student populace of the IIT’s.

As the dying dirge of the previous year draws to its cacophonic close, we make it a habit to broach upon the subject of the much debated act of ‘formulation of resolutions’. However, the element of unabashed irony here is the huge extent of inverse proportionality that exists between the time we consume in thinking up resolutions and the time we require to brutally annihilate them. I progress therefore in the utmost ‘scientific’ way to present my case with respect to the aforementioned contrast that I have stated with respect to resolutions.

Now let us assume Subject X to be the ideal and most commonplace epitome for our justification.

New Year’s Eve:

Subject X resolves to exponentially increase the quanta of work that he shall put into the forthcoming semester in order to enhance his grades and in the process redeem himself significantly in the eyes of his parents and teachers; such that they yet again restore some faith in his currently gallivanting academic potential.

Subject X also resolves to stay focussed in terms of his career goals and strike an immaculate balance between his extracurricular activities, social activities and most essentially academics.

Subject X resolves to shed those habits that have pulled him into the nether regions of his brain and digressed him from his path of motivation. In other words he resolves to tone down his acquired ‘highs’.

And most significantly Subject X resolves to go on a money-conservation spree by tethering all his unrestrained expenses of the past. His resolve stems from his new found realisation with respect to the importance of saving money (which has been instilled into his grey matter over the course of the vacations).

Within Week 2 of the New Year:

Subject X is having severe concentration issues and is deeply distracted by the likes of certain brilliant detectives, deviant chemists and other unique characters that are surfacing on the internet every time he tries to study. With the culmination of two class tests and his faring in them no better than his absence, Subject X loses the entire plot of his premier resolution.

Balance? Well that word has been shredded to infinitesimal bits as far as Subject X’s second resolution is concerned. Society, the friend circle, clubs and nights of aimless wandering are the order of the day now.

Subject X showed steely resolve and refrain during the first week of the year and this resolution seemed to be making some headway into the realm of success. But alas, with the onset of Week 2 came the downfall of all refrain and the pressure has clambered back on top. Hence welcome back old ‘highs’. The resolution however hits an all-time low.

Like a nation rocked by the rust of a recession, is the plight of Subject X within Week 2 of the year. All instilled realisations have now diffused into the collegiate air and the red carpet is rolled back on for the old friends - extravagance and well…more extravagance. The money conservation resolution thus crumbles like a pack of cards.

Subject X is now relieved of the devilish delirium of devising new revolutions for the next 11 months. Until then there is revelry and debauchery over the remains of what had once been splendid resolutions.

Resolutions In Pieces.

Ladies and Gentlemen, I therefore rest my case.
