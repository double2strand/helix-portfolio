Unto Inspiration...

In the deepest crevices of one's seemingly dark destiny, there are always uncannily hidden possibilities to derive inspiration from... It is in this situation, that mankind moves into desperation and utterly fails to derive inspiration from the most prominent source... humanity itself...

I find myself in the same diabolic dilemma wherein one step could be the fine and crafty trap that would lead to Elysium or Inferno... but I looked back and found something simple that I myself had created...and I seem to find it inspiring in its simplicity... (presuming that anyone who reads this overlooks the slight self-praise involved)...

So here goes...

' Life is truly a remarkable esplanade that itself moves on with a quaint beauty that seems to be bereft of the bounds of rationality and reason; and yet the travellers upon this esplanade never seem to hearken to the faintly fathomable splendour of life as a journey.

And due to this very reason, I prefer to step out as a traveller beyond the bounds of rationality and reason. For it is beyond these bounds, that there lies creativity...It is here wherein we give birth to the true vitality of our life.... Dreams. '

Every person holds within himself or herself the ability to summon a storm of self-belief and motivation mighty enough to even change what is ordained...

Regaining the self-belief from hereon.
